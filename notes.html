How to Succeed & Beat the Peregrine Test

Peregrine Assessment Study Guide

1

What is the Peregrine Assessment?

A comprehensive MBA exam that tests your foundational knowledge across all core business disciplines.

Exam Tip: Think broad, not deep.
2

How to Succeed

  • Understand definitions
  • Know relationships
  • Focus on business vocabulary
  • Do not memorize every formula
  • Manage your time
Textbook-standard answers are usually best.
3

Subjects Covered

  • Economics
  • Accounting
  • Finance
  • Management
  • Marketing
  • Global Business
  • Strategy
  • Legal and Ethics
  • Statistics
  • Information Systems
4

Economics Overview

Economics studies how people, businesses, and governments allocate scarce resources.

  • Supply and demand
  • Market structures
  • GDP and inflation
  • Trade and exchange rates
5

Supply and Demand

Equilibrium happens where supply equals demand.

Higher demand usually raises price when supply stays the same.
6

Price Elasticity

Elasticity measures how sensitive quantity demanded is to a price change.

  • High elasticity: customers react strongly
  • Low elasticity: customers react less
7

Market Structures

  • Perfect competition: many firms
  • Monopoly: one dominant firm
  • Oligopoly: few major firms
  • Monopolistic competition: many differentiated firms
8

GDP and Inflation

GDP is the total value of goods and services produced.

  • Nominal GDP uses current prices
  • Real GDP uses constant prices
  • Inflation is measured by CPI
9

Fiscal vs. Monetary Policy

Fiscal policy: government spending and taxes.

Monetary policy: central bank decisions about money supply and interest rates.

10

Accounting Overview

  • Income Statement: performance over time
  • Balance Sheet: financial position at one point
  • Cash Flow Statement: cash inflows and outflows
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
11

Financial Ratios

  • Liquidity: current ratio, quick ratio
  • Profitability: gross margin, ROA, ROE
  • Leverage: debt-to-equity
  • Efficiency: inventory turnover
12

Final Exam Tips

  • Questions are mostly conceptual
  • Choose the most standard answer
  • Eliminate clearly wrong choices
  • Stay calm and focused
  • Treat it like a closed-book test
