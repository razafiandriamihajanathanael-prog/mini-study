🌍 Strategic Management in a Globalized Economy

Course Learning Objectives

1

🌍 Global Strategy

Evaluate global organizational strategies using business knowledge while considering culture, laws, and economic conditions.

💡 Remember: Culture • Law • Economy
2

🏢 Company Analysis

Study a public company by understanding its history, mission, values, and business risks.

3

📊 Competitive Environment

Analyze industries, competitors, and markets to understand how global businesses compete.

4

💰 Financial Health

Use financial statements and ratios to evaluate company performance in a global economy.

5

🧠 Strategic Planning

Apply SWOT and VRIO analysis to create strong strategic recommendations.

6

🚀 Capstone Project

Combine strategy, finance, marketing, and analysis into one complete business project.
